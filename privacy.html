1. Who we are

Willow Hollow Tales is a YouTube channel with animated stories for kids, operated by Ulrich Dumez Temgoua Koudjou, Semmelweisweg 5, 65428 Rüsselsheim, Germany (the "controller"). Contact: ulrich.dumez@gmail.com. See also our Impressum (legal notice).

2. Willow Hollow Uploader and YouTube API Services

Willow Hollow Uploader is our internal tool. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload, schedule and manage videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and playlists on our own YouTube channel. Only the channel owner signs in to it.

By using a product that relies on YouTube API Services you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Which data we access

We access only data of the channel owner's own YouTube account that is needed for the tasks above: the channel ID, our own videos and their metadata, upload and processing status, thumbnails and playlists. We do not access, collect or store personal data of other YouTube users or of our viewers, and we do not collect any information from children.

4. How data is stored and shared

The authorization token for our own channel is stored securely by our integration provider Composio (Composio Inc.) solely to perform the actions described above. Our own video files and metadata are stored in our private production workspace. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, except with the service providers needed to run the tool (Google/YouTube and Composio).

5. Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the Google security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked. On request by e-mail we delete any stored data within 30 days.

6. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit it, GitHub may process your IP address in server logs for security reasons. We do not use cookies, tracking or analytics on this website.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Contact us at ulrich.dumez@gmail.com.
